🎬 Full Script with Directions

Scene 1 – Gaming Setup
Stickman is sitting, leaning forward, holding a controller. Focused expression.
“Almost… almost got the win—”
Emotion: Focused Eyes: Normal Eyebrows: Straight Mouth: Slightly open Body: Leaning forward
Sudden freeze. Screen shows “No Internet Connection”. Eyes become huge.
“NOOOOO!”
Emotion: Extreme Shock Eyes: Very Big Eyebrows: Raised High Mouth: Wide Open Body: Jumps up, both arms raised
“Not now! Not when I’m one kill away!”
Emotion: Angry Eyes: Narrowed Eyebrows: Angled Down Mouth: Open / Shouting Body: Tense, fists clenched
Scene 2 – Facing the Router
Stickman walks aggressively toward the router and points at it.
“I pay you every month… and this is how you treat me?”
Emotion: Angry + Betrayal Eyes: Narrow Eyebrows: Deep Angry Mouth: Speaking firmly Body: Pointing finger at router
WiFi suddenly comes back. Stickman freezes for 1 second. Then slowly turns to camera.
“…Okay fine. You’re forgiven. For now.”
Emotion: Relief → Funny Eyes: Soften Eyebrows: Relaxed Mouth: Big smile + tongue out Body: Turns to camera, small wave, sits back
